What Raze found
Does marking an order paid trigger anything else in your application — an email, a shipment, a fulfilment hook?
If yes, Raze never repairs unattended — it always asks you first. Repairing something that fires a shipment could send it twice.
Raze could not work out your payment model from this schema, so it will not guess. Tell it, and it will check every name against your live database before arming anything.
In order: the table your orders live in, the column holding the Razorpay order id, the column saying whether it is paid, and the column money is credited to.
Which column holds what the order should cost — as distinct from what has been credited so far?
Raze will not guess this. Two number columns look identical from the outside, and writing to the one it checks against would destroy the check. Without such a column Raze can still tell you when Razorpay and your database disagree — it just will not repair anything on its own.
On a refund, should the amount come back out of the order, or should only the status change?
Status only keeps the order's amount meaning what the customer was charged, which is what reconciliation compares against.
